What you will learn

  • Identify trees from foliage, bark, twigs and buds, reproductive structures, and whole-tree form.
  • Separate plausible look-alikes and state which visible character supports the decision.
  • Know accepted scientific names and the silvics a working forester should carry into the woods.
  • Recognize when the available evidence supports only a genus, species group, or “insufficient evidence.”

How the course will work

  • Learn at a leisurely pace or follow an intensive ten-week schedule.
  • Complete short teaching sessions, retrieval drills, multi-organ laboratories, and graded practicals.
  • Resume from any device with saved grades, mastery records, and a personal error ledger.
  • Choose whether to receive pacing prompts and useful email encouragement.

The standard

Learn the species, not the photograph.

The finished course will reward defensible field decisions, durable retrieval, and honest calibration—not confident guessing.
